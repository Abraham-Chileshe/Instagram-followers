# 🚀 Comprehensive Platform Guide

Welcome! This guide explains everything you and your users need to know about using the Instagram Growth Platform.

---

## 👤 For the User: How to Earn

### 1. Getting Started (Login)
- **Access Codes**: This website doesn't use permanent passwords. To login, you need a **Single-Use Access Code** from your recruiter or the admin. 
- **Stay Active**: Once you log in, your code is used up. If you log out or your session expires, you'll need a new code to get back in.

### 2. Posting Stories
- At the top of your dashboard, you'll see a **"Your Story"** button (just like Instagram!).
- Click the **"+"** to upload a photo or video. It will stay visible to everyone for **24 hours**.

### 3. Completing Tasks
- Look for available tasks in your dashboard (like following an account or liking a post).
- **Submit Proof**: After you finish the task on Instagram, take a screenshot and upload it using the "Submit Proof" button.
- **Get Paid**: Once an admin approves your proof, the reward is split **50-50** between you and the person who invited you.

### 4. Inviting Friends (Referrals)
- Generate an **Invite Code** from your dashboard and give it to your friends.
- When they complete tasks, you earn **passive income** (50% of their task reward!).

### 5. Withdrawing Your Money
To keep the platform healthy, there are a few rules for withdrawing:
- **The 7-Day Wait**: New accounts must wait **7 days** after joining before making their first withdrawal.
- **Activity Check**: You must have at least **one approved task** in the last 7 days to qualify.
- **Frequency**: You can only withdraw **once every 7 days**.
- **Payout Rates**: 
  - **Cash** or **Bank Transfer**: You get **100%** of your balance.
  - **USDT (Crypto)**: You get **75%** of the amount (to cover handling).

---

## 🛠 For the Admin: Running the Show

### 1. Managing Access
- **Generate Codes**: Go to the **Admin -> Codes** section to create new access keys for your users.
- **Revoking Access**: If a user unsubscribes from the target Instagram account, find their submission and click **"Revoke Access & Wipe Balance"**. This will instantly ban them and reset their earnings.

### 2. Reviewing Tasks
- Go to **Review Submissions**. 
- You will see the screenshots uploaded by users. 
- **Approve**: Adds money to the user and their recruiter.
- **Reject**: Feedback is sent to the user so they can try again.

### 3. Managing Withdrawals
- When a user requests a payout, it appears in **Withdrawals**.
- Check their payment details (Wallet address or Bank info) and process the payment manually.
- Click **Approve** once the money has been sent.

### 4. Creating New Tasks
- Use the **Task Management** section to add new Instagram posts or accounts that users need to engage with. Set the reward in AED and provide the Instagram link.
